Quantum University is a private university located in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, India. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, computer science, management, agriculture, media studies, and other disciplines.

The university focuses on industry-oriented education and practical learning. Students participate in projects, workshops, internships, and technical events to enhance their skills and prepare for professional careers.

The School of Technology at Quantum University provides programs in Computer Science Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics Engineering, and other technical fields. The curriculum includes subjects such as Data Structures, Database Management Systems, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Web Development.

The campus provides modern classrooms, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, and student activity centers. Various cultural and technical events are organized throughout the academic year to encourage innovation and teamwork among students.

Quantum University aims to develop skilled professionals by combining academic knowledge with practical experience. The institution promotes research, entrepreneurship, and innovation to help students meet the demands of the modern industry.

Roorkee, where the university is located, is an important educational city in Uttarakhand. The location provides a peaceful learning environment and good connectivity to nearby cities such as Dehradun, Haridwar, and Rishikesh.
