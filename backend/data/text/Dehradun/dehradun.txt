Dehradun is the capital city of Uttarakhand, India. It is located in the Doon Valley between the Himalayas and the Shivalik Hills. Dehradun is known for its pleasant climate, natural beauty, educational institutions, and tourist attractions.

The city serves as an important educational hub and is home to institutions such as the Forest Research Institute (FRI), Indian Military Academy (IMA), and several universities and colleges.

Popular tourist attractions in Dehradun include Robber's Cave, Sahastradhara, Mindrolling Monastery, Tapkeshwar Temple, and Forest Research Institute. The city is also a gateway to famous hill stations and pilgrimage destinations such as Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Haridwar, Kedarnath, and Badrinath.

Dehradun has a growing economy based on education, tourism, agriculture, and government services. The city is well connected by road, rail, and air through Jolly Grant Airport.

The climate of Dehradun is moderate throughout the year. Summers are warm, monsoons bring significant rainfall, and winters are cool and pleasant.

Dehradun is known for its greenery, litchi orchards, bakeries, and scenic landscapes. It attracts students, tourists, and professionals from across India.
